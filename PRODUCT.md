# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fraser Valley locals and Highway 1 travellers deciding where to eat in
Abbotsford. They find Pho Abby on Google (4.6 stars, 435 reviews), tap through
on a phone, and want three things fast: is it open, what's on the menu, how do I
order or get there. Secondary: the owners, evaluating a pitch site from Destura.

## Product Purpose

A one-page marketing site for **Pho Abby**, a family-run Vietnamese restaurant at
2100 Whatcom Rd, Abbotsford BC, open since February 2024. Success = a visitor
calls, starts an order, or gets directions. The page must feel like the room:
warm, quick, unpretentious, generous portions.

Status: **pitch / preview build.** Menu beyond the starters is realistic
placeholder content, flagged in code, to be replaced with the real menu. Not
indexed. Ordering links point at phone / website until verified URLs arrive.

## Brand Personality

**Warm · Quick · Unfussy.** A neighbourhood kitchen, not a concept restaurant.
The broth is simmered from early morning; the service is fast; the bowl is too
big to finish. Copy is plainspoken and a little proud. No hype, no "authentic
journey" cliche in the final copy.

## Anti-references

- Vietnamese-restaurant category reflex: fire-engine red + flag-yellow, or bamboo
  / lotus / conical-hat decoration, or a full warm-cream "cozy" wash.
- AI-generated restaurant tells: cream/beige body background, eyebrow kicker on
  every section, 01/02/03 section numbers, identical menu cards, hero carousels.
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, side-stripe borders, decorative status dots.
- Stock-photo gloss. Food imagery should look close, warm, and real.

## Design Principles

1. **The menu is the product.** It must be scannable in one pass — group heading,
   item, price, done. Everything else supports it.
2. **Committed warmth from the brand colour, not the canvas.** Pure white page;
   a deep lacquer-amber does the work on frames, headings, CTAs, footer.
3. **One page, a clear beat.** White editorial sections alternate with 1-2
   full-bleed dark-amber frames so scrolling has rhythm.
4. **One motion signature: rising steam** over the hero, tied to the emotional
   job (a hot bowl). Visible by default; full reduced-motion off-switch.
   Everything else is hover / focus / active feedback only.
5. **Honest content.** Confirmed facts only in prose (Feb 2024 opening, address,
   hours, real starters, real review quotes). Everything unconfirmed is a
   visible `TODO` in code, never a silent guess.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA throughout: body >= 4.5:1, large text >= 3:1, white-on-amber button
>= 4.5:1. `prefers-reduced-motion` replaces the steam with a static glow.
Keyboard navigable, visible focus rings, skip link, `tel:` tap target >= 44px on
mobile, hours `<dl>` with today's row marked in text as well as colour.
