Vietnamese restaurant

Located in Abbotsford,
cooked like home.

Phở Abby grew out of a love for Vietnamese cooking. Hien's family opened it on Whatcom Road in 2023, working from recipes passed down through generations: the signature phở alongside a few modern twists, each dish made with care.

People pull off the highway for a quick bowl on the way through. Families take the long table by the window and stay a while.
